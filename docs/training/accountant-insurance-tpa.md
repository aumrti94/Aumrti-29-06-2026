# Accountant / Insurance-TPA Executive — Training Manual

**Modules:** Financial Accounts (`src/pages/accounts/AccountsPage.tsx` and six standalone
`/accounts/*` pages) and Insurance & TPA (`src/pages/insurance/InsurancePage.tsx`)
**Owner:** Ashok (accounts/ERP/Tally) for the Accounts half, Pooja (insurance/TPA/pre-auth) for
the Insurance half
**Screens:** `Accounts` and `Insurance` in the main menu

> Money on every screen in this manual is shown in Indian Rupees with the ₹ symbol and Indian
> digit grouping (lakhs/crores). Dates are shown as DD/MM/YYYY. You never type a GST percentage —
> it is picked up automatically from your hospital's configured rates. You never type a journal
> entry number or a bill number by hand — both are generated by the system.

**This file does not cover generating or collecting a patient's bill.** That is the Billing
Clerk's day-to-day job, already documented in
[billing-clerk.md](./billing-clerk.md) — this file starts from where a bill becomes an accounting
entry or an insurance claim, not from where it's created. If you need to open a patient's bill,
take payment, or process a refund, see that file instead.

**Job-title note, confirmed against `src/lib/routeRoles.ts`:** "Accountant / Insurance-TPA
Executive" as a single combined job title does not map to a single login role today. See
**Access control** at the end of this file before assuming one login covers everything below —
it does not, for every combination of screens.

## Part A — Accounts (Ashok)

### What this role does in the system

Open **Accounts** from the main menu — you land on `src/pages/accounts/AccountsPage.tsx`, a
header bar plus a row of tabs:

**Dashboard · Ledger · Expenses · Journal · Bank · Reports**

The header also carries three buttons that are not tabs — they either open a standalone page or a
modal:
- **Cost Centres** — navigates to the standalone `/accounts/cost-centres` page.
- **Record Expense** — opens a modal to log a one-off expense (the same form that lives inside
  the **Expenses** tab).
- **Export**.

A date-range dropdown (This Month / Last Month / This Quarter / This Year) at the top scopes every
tab except Bank.

Six further screens exist as their own full pages outside this tab bar, reached by URL or by a
button elsewhere in Settings/Accounts — **Opening Balances** (`/accounts/setup`), **Chart of
Accounts** (`/accounts/chart-of-accounts`), **Journal Workbench** (`/accounts/journal-workbench`),
**Cost Centres** (`/accounts/cost-centres`), **Financial Statements**
(`/accounts/financial-statements`), **Budget Management** (`/accounts/budget`), and **Fixed
Assets Register** (`/accounts/fixed-assets`). Each is covered below.

Your day-to-day work as Accountant is:
- Reviewing the automatic journal entries every billing/payroll/expense transaction already
  posted for you (**Journal** tab), and posting the rare manual entry yourself (**Journal
  Workbench**).
- Logging one-off expenses with their GST and payment mode (**Expenses** tab).
- Reconciling the bank statement against the ledger (**Bank** tab).
- Pulling P&L, Balance Sheet, GSTR, TDS, and Tally exports for the month (**Reports** tab, and the
  standalone **Financial Statements** page).
- Keeping the Chart of Accounts, Cost Centres, Budget, and Fixed Assets Register up to date.

You never post a manual journal entry for a routine billing, payment, or payroll transaction — the
system does that automatically the moment the underlying transaction happens. Manual journal
entries (via **Journal Workbench**) are for adjustments, accruals, and corrections only.

---

### A1. Read the Dashboard

1. Open **Accounts** — you land on **Dashboard** by default.
2. Five KPI cards across the top: **Revenue This Month**, **Expenses This Month**, **Net
   Profit/Loss** (with margin %), **Accounts Receivable**, **Cash & Bank Balance**. These respect
   the date-range dropdown in the header.
3. Below that: a **Monthly Revenue vs Expenses** area chart (trailing 6 real calendar months,
   independent of the date filter) and a **Revenue Breakdown** donut by department (OPD, IPD, OT,
   Lab, Radiology, Pharmacy IP/Retail, Procedures, Emergency, Insurance, PMJAY/CGHS, Other).
4. Below that: **Expense Categories** (bar chart by Salaries/Drug Purchases/Rent/Utilities/
   Maintenance/Other) and **Recent Journal Entries** (last 10, each tagged **AUTO** or **MANUAL**).
5. If your hospital has no auto-posting rules configured yet, an amber banner appears at the top
   warning that journal entries will not be created for bills, payments, or payroll until
   **Settings → Accounting → Auto-Posting Rules** is set up — dismiss it with the **×**, or go fix
   it before relying on this dashboard's numbers.
6. Further down, a **Posting Failures** card lists any transaction that should have posted a
   journal entry but didn't (no matching auto-posting rule) — review and resolve these, they are
   real gaps in your books. A **Financial Anomaly Detector** card follows it.

### A2. Browse the Ledger and look up an account's history

1. Go to the **Ledger** tab. You see every account in the Chart of Accounts: **Code, Account
   Name, Type** (coloured badge: asset/liability/equity/revenue/expense), **Subtype**.
2. Use the **search box** or the **type filter** dropdown to narrow the list.
3. Click any account row to open its own transaction history — a running-balance table showing
   every journal line posted against it: **Date, Entry #, Description, Debit, Credit, Balance**.
4. Click **← Back to Chart of Accounts** to return to the list.

This is a read-only drill-down. To change which accounts exist, use the standalone **Chart of
Accounts** page (A7 below).

### A3. Log an expense

1. Go to the **Expenses** tab (or click **Record Expense** in the header — same form, as a modal).
2. Fill in: **Expense Date**, **Expense Category** (Salaries, Rent, Electricity, Water, Telephone,
   Internet, Fuel, Vehicle, Equipment Maintenance, Building Maintenance, Housekeeping, Insurance,
   Professional Fees, Marketing, Printing & Stationery, Bank Charges, Miscellaneous, Other),
   **Vendor / Payee**, **Description**, **Amount (₹)**, **GST (₹)** (the **Total** box
   auto-calculates and is read-only), **Payment Mode** (💵 Cash / 🏦 Bank Transfer / 📜 Cheque /
   📱 UPI / 💳 Card), an optional **Reference Number**, an optional **Department (cost centre)**,
   and an optional **Receipt Upload** (jpg/png/pdf, max 5MB).
3. Click **Save Expense**. The system posts the matching journal entry for you automatically — the
   confirmation toast names the entry number (e.g. "Expense recorded ₹X — JE ...").
4. The right-hand panel lists every expense already logged, filterable by **All / This Week / This
   Month** and by category, with a running **Total** and an **Export Excel** button. Each row with
   an uploaded receipt shows a **View** link.

### A4. Review and reverse journal entries (Journal tab)

1. Go to the **Journal** tab. This is a **view and reverse** screen for entries already posted —
   it is not where you create a new manual entry (see A5 for that).
2. Filter by date range, by **All / Auto / Manual** type chips, by account, or search by entry
   number or description.
3. Click any row to expand it and see its line items (account code, account name, debit, credit).
4. Entries tagged **AUTO** were posted automatically by a billing/payment/payroll transaction;
   **MANUAL** entries were posted by someone on this page or via Journal Workbench; **REVERSAL**
   entries are system-generated offsets of a reversed entry.
5. If an entry needs correcting, click **Reverse** on its row (only shown for entries the system
   judges safe to reverse — manual or bill-anchored auto entries; a reversal is rejected with a
   clear toast for entries touching subledgers like fixed assets, depreciation, or inventory).
   Confirm the prompt — this posts a new balanced entry with debits and credits swapped, and marks
   the original as **REVERSED**. You never retrospectively edit or delete a posted entry.
6. The bottom bar always shows running **Total Debits** / **Total Credits** and a
   **✓ Balanced** / **✗ Unbalanced** badge for whatever is currently filtered into view.

### A5. Post a manual journal entry (Journal Workbench)

The **Journal** tab above is read/reverse only. To post a brand-new manual entry — an accrual,
adjustment, or correction that isn't a routine billing/payment/payroll transaction — use the
standalone **Journal Workbench** page (`/accounts/journal-workbench`), reached by URL or from
wherever your hospital has linked it (e.g. a Settings or Accounts menu entry).

1. On **Journal Workbench**, click **Add New** (top right of the "Recent Journal Entries" list).
2. A **"New Manual Journal Entry"** window opens. Enter the **Date** and a **Description /
   Narration**.
3. For each line, pick an **Account** from the dropdown and enter either a **Debit (₹)** or
   **Credit (₹)** amount (entering one clears the other on that line). Click **+ Add line** for
   more lines; the trash icon removes a line (minimum two lines).
4. A live balance indicator shows **Debit: ₹X | Credit: ₹Y** and either **✓ Balanced** or
   **Off by ₹Z**. The **Post Journal Entry** button stays disabled until debits equal credits.
5. Click **Post Journal Entry**. The entry number is generated by the system
   (`JE-<year>-<sequence>`, via the shared `next_seq` database sequence) — you never type one.

### A6. Reconcile the bank account

1. Go to the **Bank** tab. Pick a bank account from the dropdown (configured in Settings if none
   appear).
2. **Import Bank Statement** — click the dashed upload box, choose a CSV (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis,
   Kotak, PNB statement exports are supported), then map its columns (Date, Description, Debit,
   Credit) in the **"Map CSV Columns"** window and click **Import N Transactions**.
3. Or click **Add Transaction** to key in a single line manually (date, description, debit,
   credit, reference), then **Save**.
4. Enter the bank's own **Opening Balance** figure in the box provided — the reconciliation summary
   (closing balance, uncleared deposits, outstanding cheques, adjusted book balance, difference)
   calculates from this plus the imported/entered transactions.
5. Click **Auto-Match** to let the system pair unreconciled bank lines against ledger journal
   lines on the cash/bank accounts (same amount, within 2 days) — it reports how many it matched.
6. For anything Auto-Match misses, click **Match** on that row to open a manual matching list of
   candidate journal lines and confirm the right one.
7. The **Unreconciled (N)** / **Reconciled (N)** tabs on the right show you what's left to do.

### A7. Chart of Accounts — view only (content gap, stated plainly)

The standalone **Chart of Accounts** page (`/accounts/chart-of-accounts`) lists every ledger
account (**Code, Account Name, Type, Subtype, Status**) with search. Grounding this in
`src/pages/accounts/ChartOfAccountsPage.tsx`: its **Add New** button does not open a form — the
`onAdd` handler is `console.log("Add new account clicked")` only. There is currently no working UI
to add a new account from this screen. If your hospital needs a new ledger account, it has to be
added at the database/migration level — this is a real content gap, not a step this manual is
omitting; flag it to Ashok/your implementation team rather than hunting for a hidden "+" button.

### A8. Cost Centres — department spend vs budget

1. From the **Accounts** header, click **Cost Centres** (or go to `/accounts/cost-centres`
   directly). Pick a period from the dropdown (This Month / Last Month / This Quarter / This
   Year).
2. The table lists every department as a **Cost Centre** with its **Actual Spend** (pulled live
   from journal line items tagged with that department as cost centre, expense accounts only),
   an editable **Monthly Budget (₹)** box, computed **Variance**, a **Utilisation** bar, and a
   **Status** badge (On Track / Over).
3. Type a budget figure into any row's box — it saves as soon as you click away (on blur).

**Content note:** per the page's own footer text and the source (`CostCentresPage.tsx`), budget
figures here are saved to **browser `localStorage`**, keyed to this hospital and department — not
to a database table. They will not appear on another device or browser, and will not survive
clearing site data. For a budget that needs to persist centrally and go through an approval chain,
use **Budget Management** (A10) instead, which is database-backed.

### A9. Financial Statements (standalone) vs the Reports tab — what's different

There are two places to read a P&L and Balance Sheet, and they are not the same screen:

- **Reports tab** (inside Accounts) has nine sub-tabs: **P&L Statement, Balance Sheet, Trial
  Balance, Account Statement, Department P&L, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, TDS, Tally Export**. This is the
  fuller tool — it also drives your statutory GST/TDS filings and the Tally XML export (A12).
- **Financial Statements** (`/accounts/financial-statements`, standalone page) shows only **P&L
  Statement** and **Balance Sheet** — the same two statements, computed from the same ledger
  (`fetchLedgerBalances`/`buildProfitAndLoss`/`buildBalanceSheet` in `src/lib/financialStatements.ts`)
  — but adds a **cost-centre filter** on the P&L (the Reports tab's P&L does not filter by cost
  centre) and a one-click **Export** to CSV. The Balance Sheet is always shown at entity level
  regardless of the cost-centre filter — a note on screen says so.
- If the Balance Sheet does not tie (Assets ≠ Liabilities + Equity), Financial Statements shows a
  red banner naming the exact difference — chase down unbalanced journal entries via the Journal
  tab (A4) when you see this.

Use **Reports → P&L/Balance Sheet** for monthly closing and statutory work; use the standalone
**Financial Statements** page when you specifically need the cost-centre-filtered P&L or a quick
CSV.

1. On the Reports tab (inside Accounts), the **P&L Statement** shows Revenue from Operations by
   department, Other Income, Cost of Services, Gross Profit, Personnel/Infrastructure/Maintenance/
   Administrative/Non-Cash expense groups, and Net Profit/Loss — each with **Print**, **PDF**, and
   **Excel** buttons. An **AI Financial Analysis** button (requires an AI provider configured in
   Settings → API Configuration Hub) produces a 5-point written analysis.
2. **Balance Sheet** is a point-in-time snapshot (always cumulative from inception to the period
   end, never just the filtered period) — Assets on the left, Liabilities & Equity on the right,
   same Print/Excel options.
3. **Trial Balance**, **Account Statement**, and **Department P&L** sub-tabs give you a raw debit/
   credit list, a single account's full transaction history, and department-wise profitability.
4. **GSTR-1** and **GSTR-3B** sub-tabs compute your outward-tax and ITC figures straight from
   billed GST line items — this is where your GST filing numbers come from, not a separate manual
   calculation.
5. **TDS** sub-tab lists TDS-applicable expense categories (Professional Fees — Section 194J,
   Rent — 194I, Contractors — 194C) with computed TDS amounts.
6. **Tally Export** sub-tab builds a Tally-compatible XML voucher bundle (Sales, Receipts,
   Purchases, Journals — pick which to include) and either downloads it or emails it; every export
   is logged.

### A10. Budget Management

1. Go to `/accounts/budget`. Pick a **Fiscal Year** (2024-25 / 2025-26 / 2026-27) and optionally a
   **Month**.
2. Click **Add Line** to create a budget line: **Account Code**, **Account Name**, optional
   **Month** (blank = annual), **Budgeted Amount (₹)**, optional **Notes**. Click **Add**.
3. New lines start as **draft**. Click **Submit** on a draft row to move it to
   **pending approval**.
4. If your role is `cfo`, `hospital_admin`, or `super_admin`, **Approve**/**Reject** buttons appear
   on pending rows — a plain Accountant login cannot approve its own budget lines, only submit
   them.
5. Click **Refresh Actuals** at any time to pull real spend from the general ledger into every
   budget line's **Actual** column — **Variance** recalculates automatically.
6. **Export** downloads the current fiscal year/month view as CSV.

### A11. Fixed Assets Register

1. Go to `/accounts/fixed-assets`. Four tabs: **Asset Register, Depreciation, Insurance,
   Disposal**.
2. Click **Add Asset** to register one: **Asset Code**, **Asset Name**, **Category** (Medical
   Equipment / Furniture & Fixtures / IT Equipment / Vehicle / Building / Land / Other),
   **Purchase Date**, **Purchase Cost (₹)**, **Salvage Value (₹)**, **Useful Life (years)**,
   **Depreciation Method** (Straight Line / Written Down Value), **Funding Source** (Bank /
   Accounts Payable-Vendor / Capital-Owner Funds), plus optional Location, Vendor, Serial No.,
   Warranty/AMC expiry, Invoice No., and Insurance fields.
3. Tick **"Pre-existing asset (opening balance)"** only for an asset the hospital already owned
   before going live on Aumrti — this books its cost and already-accumulated depreciation against
   Capital with no cash or P&L impact, instead of a fresh acquisition entry.
4. Click **Add Asset**. This books the matching journal entry (acquisition or opening) to the
   general ledger automatically, so it appears on the Balance Sheet.
5. Click **Run Depreciation** whenever you need this month's depreciation posted — it is idempotent
   (safe to click more than once; it won't double-post).
6. The **Depreciation** tab shows every asset's accumulated depreciation and book value; the
   **Insurance** tab tracks policy expiry; the **Disposal** tab handles writing an asset off the
   register. **Export** downloads the current register as CSV.

### A12. Opening Balances (one-time, first go-live only)

`/accounts/setup` is a 6-step wizard — **Opening Date, Cash & Bank, Receivables, Payables, Assets &
Inventory, Loans** — that posts a single opening-balance journal entry when a hospital first goes
live on Aumrti. It is **idempotent by design**: the page itself blocks a second run once one
opening-balance entry exists, with a toast telling you to reverse the existing one via **Accounts →
Journal** first if the figures were wrong. You will almost never touch this page outside initial
go-live.

## Part B — Insurance & TPA (Pooja)

### What this role does in the system

Open **Insurance** from the main menu — `src/pages/insurance/InsurancePage.tsx` is a left-hand
navigation (not a top tab bar) grouped into four sections, confirmed against the `navGroups` array
in source:

- **Pre-Admission & Admission** — Active Admissions, Intimations, **Pre-Auth Queue**,
  Enhancement Queue (Enhancement Queue is restricted to `insurance_executive`/`super_admin`/
  `hospital_admin`).
- **Claim Processing** — **Claims to Submit**, HCX Claims, **Claims Status**, Denial Management,
  TPA Queries, **TPA Ageing**, Unified View, **Reconciliation**, TPA Disputes.
- **Government Schemes** — CGHS / ECHS, ESI Scheme, Arogyasri / State.
- **Operations & Analytics** — Denial Analytics, Automation, Auto Settings, TPA Configuration,
  Plan & Settings.

A KPI strip sits above the nav on every screen: **Pre-Auth Pending**, **Claims Outstanding**,
**Denied This Period**, **TPA Queries Overdue**, **Need Supplementary**, **Automation Rate**, with
a collapsible second row (**Avg Pre-Auth Time, First-pass Approval, Avg Claim Settlement,
Underpayments Pending, Recovery Rate**). Clicking most KPI cards jumps you straight to the related
screen. A plan-tier badge (Plan A — Manual / Plan B — AI Assisted / Plan C — Automated) shows your
hospital's current insurance automation tier — it changes what buttons are available on Claims to
Submit (see B2).

Your day-to-day work is working admissions through pre-authorisation, submitting claims once a
patient is discharged, chasing claim status and TPA queries, and reconciling what the TPA actually
paid against what was claimed.

### B1. Work the Pre-Auth Queue

1. Click **Pre-Auth Queue** in the left nav.
2. For an admission with insurance, an **intimation window alert** banner shows how many hours
   remain to submit intimation (24 hours for emergency, 48 hours for planned) — red if the window
   has passed, amber if under 6 hours remain, green otherwise. Submit intimation before this window
   closes to avoid claim rejection later.
3. Fill in the pre-auth form: TPA, policy number, estimated amount, procedure/diagnosis codes
   (searchable via the built-in ICD-10/procedure code search), and — for an accident/trauma case —
   tick the MLC/FIR fields.
4. A **Policy Verification** panel and a **Document Checklist** (readiness count) sit alongside
   the form — both should be green/complete before you submit.
5. Once a TPA responds, click to record their decision: **Approved / Partially Approved /
   Rejected**, with the approved amount, pre-auth reference number, and validity date (or a denial
   reason if rejected).

### B2. Submit a claim (Claims to Submit)

1. Click **Claims to Submit**. The table lists discharged-patient bills that are eligible for a
   claim but don't have one yet — columns **Patient, Bill #, TPA, Amount, Pre-Auth, Denial Risk,
   Submit By, Action**. A **Submit By** badge turns red once you're within 7 days of the 60-day
   submission deadline from discharge.
2. Per row, click **Bundle** to assemble the claim document pack, or **AI Review** to get an AI
   denial-risk score before submitting (shows as "AI: NN%" once run).
3. What happens next depends on your hospital's **plan tier** (shown in the header badge):
   - **Plan A — Manual**: a **✓ Submit** button appears once AI Review has run; if the AI score is
     above 50% you must explicitly confirm the high-risk warning before the button becomes clickable.
   - Plan B/C add further automated submission options alongside Submit.
4. Click **+ New Claim** (top right) to start a claim manually via the **Claims Pack Wizard**,
   instead of waiting for a bill to appear in this list.

### B3. Track and action claim status (Claims Status)

1. Click **Claims Status**. Filter chips across the top: **All / submitted / under_review /
   approved / partially_approved / rejected / settled / written_off**.
2. The table shows **Claim #, Patient, TPA, Claimed, Approved, Status, Days, Actions**. Status
   badges carry extra context: an **IRDAI** badge counts down the regulatory 45-day settlement
   clock on submitted/under-review claims; **Appeal by** and **Resubmit by** badges appear on
   rejected claims.
3. Row actions, shown per status:
   - **📋 Record Decision** — on any non-final claim, to log what the TPA actually decided.
   - **🖨️ Receipt** — on approved/settled/partially-approved claims, prints a TPA receipt.
   - **📋 Log Rejection** (or **Update Rejection**) — on a rejected claim, to capture the rejection
     code, the TPA's exact wording, their reference number, and the notice date. Saving this sets
     the appeal deadline automatically to 30 days from the notice date.
   - **⚖️ Appeal** — only enabled once a rejection code or reason is logged — opens the appeal
     letter drafting flow.
   - **Resubmit** — opens a resubmission sheet for a corrected re-claim.
4. "Denial Management" in the left nav is the same **Claims Status** screen pre-filtered to
   `rejected` — `InsurancePage.tsx` renders `<ClaimsStatus initialFilter="rejected" />` for that
   nav item, it is not a separate component.

### B4. Chase ageing claims (TPA Ageing)

1. Click **TPA Ageing**. Four KPI cards: **Total Outstanding**, **Overdue (>60 days)**, **Avg
   Settlement Days** (target 45 days), **This Month Settled**.
2. A bar chart and a table break outstanding claim value down per TPA into **0-30 / 31-60 / 61-90 /
   90+ days** buckets.
3. Click the follow-up action on a TPA's row to open a pre-filled WhatsApp message quoting their
   outstanding total and asking them to expedite settlement.

### B5. Reconcile what the TPA actually paid (Reconciliation)

1. Click **Reconciliation**. Three tabs: **Pending (N)**, **KPI Dashboard**, **TPA Performance**.
2. On **Pending**, the left list shows every claim awaiting reconciliation, oldest first, with an
   urgency flag past 30 days. Click one to open its detail panel.
3. If no payment is recorded yet, fill in **Record TPA Payment**: **TPA Payment Amount (₹)**,
   **Payment Date**, **TPA Advice Reference #**, **Bank Reference**, then click **Record Payment**.
   A live preview shows whether this is an underpayment, overpayment, or fully settled before you
   save.
4. Once a payment is recorded, the panel shows **Payment Recorded** details and the exact
   difference. For an underpayment you can either:
   - **Accept Underpayment — Write Off ₹X**, or
   - **Raise Dispute** — opens a dispute form (reason, notes, and an AI-drafted dispute letter
     option) to send back to the TPA.
5. **KPI Dashboard** and **TPA Performance** tabs give aggregate settlement-speed and approval-rate
   numbers per TPA — useful for escalating a consistently slow or low-paying TPA.

### B6. Government schemes and remaining screens

**CGHS / ECHS**, **ESI Scheme**, and **Arogyasri / State** each have their own tab for that
scheme's specific workflow (government-scheme patients are billed and claimed differently from
commercial TPA business — these are not the same form as the TPA screens above). **HCX Claims**,
**TPA Queries**, **Unified View**, **TPA Disputes**, **Denial Analytics**, **Automation**, **Auto
Settings**, and **TPA Configuration** are further screens in the same nav groups, each scoped to
its own named workflow — not walked through step-by-step here, as this manual's required coverage
is Pre-Auth Queue → Claims to Submit → Claims Status / TPA Ageing → Reconciliation per the brief
that scoped this file. **Plan & Settings** is where your hospital's insurance automation tier
(Plan A/B/C) and SLA alert channel are configured — not a daily-use screen for this role.

## Access control — confirmed against `src/lib/routeRoles.ts`

Re-checked directly against the current file (not carried over from memory):

```
Line 16: ROUTE_ROLES['/accounts']   = ['accountant', 'billing_executive', 'billing_staff', 'cfo', 'super_admin', 'hospital_admin'];
Line 20: ROUTE_ROLES['/insurance']  = ['billing_executive', 'insurance_executive', 'super_admin', 'hospital_admin'];
Line 59: ROUTE_ROLES['/accounts/financial-statements'] = ['super_admin', 'hospital_admin', 'cfo', 'accountant'];
Line 60: ROUTE_ROLES['/accounts/budget']               = ['super_admin', 'hospital_admin', 'cfo', 'accountant'];
Line 61: ROUTE_ROLES['/accounts/fixed-assets']         = ['super_admin', 'hospital_admin', 'cfo', 'accountant'];
```

This confirms the brief's assumptions exactly, with one consequence worth stating plainly:

- A login with role **`accountant`** reaches `/accounts` and all of Part A above, including
  Financial Statements, Budget Management, and Fixed Assets (lines 59–61 explicitly include
  `accountant`). It does **not** reach `/insurance` — `accountant` is not in that route's role
  list — so an `accountant` login cannot do any of Part B.
- A login with role **`insurance_executive`** reaches `/insurance` and all of Part B. It is **not**
  in the `/accounts` role list at all, so it cannot reach any Accounts screen, including the basic
  `AccountsPage.tsx` tabs (Dashboard/Ledger/Expenses/Journal/Bank/Reports) — not just the
  CFO-gated sub-pages.
- A login with role **`billing_executive`** is the only role that reaches *both* `/accounts` and
  `/insurance` — covering Part A's `AccountsPage.tsx` tabs and all of Part B — but `billing_executive`
  is **not** in the line 59–61 list, so that same login is blocked from Financial Statements,
  Budget Management, and Fixed Assets Register. A `billing_executive` login doing both halves of
  this job title still cannot complete A9's standalone Financial Statements page, A10's Budget
  Management, or A11's Fixed Assets Register — only `accountant`/`cfo`/`hospital_admin`/
  `super_admin` can.
- **`billing_staff`** reaches `/accounts` (same tier as `billing_executive`, per line 16) but is
  not in the `/insurance` list and not in the CFO-gated sub-page list either.

**Net effect — stated the way Phase 2 stated its RBAC gaps:** there is no single login role today
that cleanly covers "Accountant / Insurance-TPA Executive" as one combined job title end to end. If
one person is meant to do everything in this file, they need either an `accountant` login (full
Accounts, zero Insurance) plus a second `insurance_executive` login, or a `billing_executive` login
(full Accounts tabs + full Insurance, but no Financial Statements/Budget/Fixed Assets). This is
